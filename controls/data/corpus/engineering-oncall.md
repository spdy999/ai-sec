# On-call rotation

Classification: internal
Audience: all-staff

The platform rota runs Monday 09:00 to Monday 09:00, one primary and one
secondary. Paging goes to the primary first and escalates to the secondary
after ten minutes without acknowledgement.

Severity 1 means customer-facing unavailability and is acknowledged within
fifteen minutes at any hour. Severity 3 waits for business hours.

Handover happens in the Monday rota call. The outgoing primary writes a short
note covering anything still open.
