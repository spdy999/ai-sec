# Leave policy

Classification: internal
Audience: all-staff

Employees accrue 2.5 days of paid leave per completed month of service.
Unused leave carries over to 31 March of the following year, after which it
lapses. Requests of five consecutive days or more need line-manager approval
at least two weeks ahead.

Sick leave does not draw down the paid-leave balance. A medical certificate is
required from the third consecutive day.
